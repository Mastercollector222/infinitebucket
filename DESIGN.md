# Design system — InfiniteBucket

Reference: public/logo.png
Object: brushed metallic amethyst-violet bucket, infinity-loop handle, embossed infinity, white wordmark.

## Direction
Luxury product site, not a meme landing page.
Think: dark Apple product page × live trading terminal.
Active, not busy. Motion is purposeful.

## Color
--void:        #07040C
--obsidian:    #0E0816
--panel:       #161022
--panel-2:     #1C142C
--stroke:      rgba(196,160,255,0.18)
--violet-900:  #2A1248
--violet-700:  #5B2A9A
--violet-500:  #8B5CF6
--amethyst:    #B57BFF
--chrome:      #E8D7FF
--white:       #F7F4FF
--muted:       #9B92B3
--buy:         #3DDC97
--sell:        #FF5D7A
--gold:        #E2C48A

Backgrounds: near-black with violet bloom, not flat purple fills.
Metal = gradients + hairline highlights, not a purple box.

## Type
Display: "Syne" or "Geist" extra-bold, tight tracking
Body: "Geist" or "Satoshi"/Inter
Mono (CA, stats): "JetBrains Mono" or "Geist Mono"
Hero line-height 0.92. Never all-caps paragraphs.

## Motion
- Infinity ribbon slowly orbiting behind the hero bucket
- Live ticker tape under the nav
- Stat numbers count up once on enter, then tick with real data
- Soft metallic sheen that travels across the logo every 8s
- Trade tape: new rows slide in
- Reduced-motion: disable all of the above
- No confetti, no matrix rain, no 3D tilt on every card

## Layout
Max content 1280px. Huge margins. One idea per section.
Mobile: stack, CA copy still one tap, buy CTA sticky.
